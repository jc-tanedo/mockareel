# Mockareel 0.14.0

Create a simulated Street View journey from your own photos, videos and supported GIFs, with editable Maps and Street View interfaces.

## What's new

- **Streetview journey template:** begin on a captured Maps screen, tap its media thumbnail, then enter a Street View scene. Each media item has its own location, editable overlays and transition settings, which stay attached when reordered.
- **Two journey transitions:** directional left/right arrow movement with tap ripples and motion blur, or return to Maps at the next media's location. Opening maps, later maps, media and transitions have separate timing controls. Optionally finish on the last location's map.
- **Camera motion:** combine slow zoom, pan and simulated turntable movement. Navigation arrows move with the scene while the toolbar and place sheet stay fixed.
- **Media effects:** choose fisheye or pincushion with adjustable strength. Photos support up to eight movable blur spots with larger touch targets and a magnifier while dragging. Videos and GIFs support lens effects; blur spots are photo-only. Originals and editable recipes remain in projects and backups.
- **Clearer editor controls:** shared settings cards replace collapsible sections across the editors. Camera motion, Street View overlays and journey timing are distinct sections, with consistent headings and slider alignment.
- **FYP home screens:** iPhone, Android, Pixel and Samsung One UI presets, editable app artwork and mock status/widget fields, plus a supplied-media mode that hides duplicate overlays.

## Fixes and improvements

- Browser map capture uses a larger internal map and crops out its built-in controls before the template adds its own interface. Capture no longer waits indefinitely after map tiles are ready.
- Enter map/imagery credits in the framing screen; the app adds Google Maps branding and those credits to the saved PNG after extraction. That same asset is used in preview, export and backups.
- Streetview preview and export share one timeline, including ripple timing, transition direction, motion, video/GIF playback and the exact ending.
- The project library distinguishes **Maps Story**, **Street View**, and **FYP Scroll** projects.
- Debug APKs use a stable signing key, and persistent build runners reuse local toolchains instead of restoring large remote cache archives on every job.

## Install and existing projects

Download `mockareel-0.14.0-release.apk` and its SHA-256 checksum from this release. This is the normal **Mockareel** app (`dev.mockareel.mockareel`), built with the configured production signing key. **Mockareel Debug** is a separate app; transfer its projects with **Share project backup**, then import the `.mockareel` files into the release app.

Existing Maps and TikTok projects retain their template settings and behavior. Captured maps, uploaded media, music and editable effects are included in portable backups. To remove controls already baked into an older map image, capture that map again; upgrading cannot remove them from the saved PNG.

Streetview uses ordinary media rather than interactive 360° panoramas. Transitions through Maps do not animate geographic travel. Live browser map rendering and available place details can vary. See the [Streetview guide](https://github.com/jc-tanedo/mockareel/blob/v0.14.0/docs/streetview.md).

Validation includes formatting, Dart analysis, 114 Flutter tests, 12 native Android tests and 21 CI helper tests. Phone/tablet settings renders were visually reviewed. The release workflow also checks the signed APK's package/version, ZIP integrity, 16 KB alignment and credential patterns before publishing the APK and checksum. Device testing remains necessary for hardware-specific media behavior.
